   - Auto-open delegated to `ProjectPlansController.loadFolders` upon resolving `response.kind == PlanFolderKind.PLAN`.
   - Unit test `testProjectPlansControllerAutoOpensPlanOnPlanKindFolder` verifies exactly 1 plan fetch is made without duplicate or recursive fetches.
2. **Workspace Purge Dialog Destructive Scope**:
   - `PurgeHistoryDialog` updated in `WorkflowModals.kt`: title explicitly states "Purge All Workspace History".
   - Dialog body explicitly warns that all activity events and soft-deleted notes across all projects in the workspace will be permanently deleted.
   - Added RFC3339 timestamp validation on confirm button to prevent accidental blank/malformed purges.
