No fixture-source changes.
3. Resolve launchers with `realpath` before mounting. Resolve OMP repository from `dirname(realpath(command -v omp))/../../..`; observed dev wrapper needs that repository mounted at the **same absolute path**, including dependencies. Native binaries can be mounted directly at `/opt/qualification-bin/codex` and `/opt/qualification-bin/claude`; Bun at `/opt/qualification-bin/bun`, all `ro=true`.

Mount exact binary files at their bin paths as above (or symlink within temporary bin dir to their container-mounted destinations). Do not mount user global agent/config directories wholesale.

